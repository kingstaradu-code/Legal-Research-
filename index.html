Anti-hallucination rules (applies to content added to this site)

  1. Never invent a case, citation, court, date, or holding. If the author is not highly confident a case is real and the citation accurate, state that explicitly.
  2. If unsure about a citation, mark it as unverified and point users to BAILII or GhaLII for confirmation rather than presenting a guess.
  3. Do not pad lists to hit a requested number; only publish verified cases.
  4. Do not mix real names with invented citations. Each field must come from the same verified source.
  5. Do not summarise a case you have not verified; prefer no summary to an invented one.
  6. For every case added, include where it should be verified (e.g., "check this against BAILII" or "check this against GhaLII").
  7. Requests for "every case" should be answered by explaining scale and limits rather than attempting exhaustive lists.

Try a legal concept ("promissory estoppel"), a statute name ("Evidence Act, 1975 (NRCD 323)"), or a case name.

Direct to the full archives

No single database has "every" case — these two come closest, free.

BAILII holds the most complete free archive of English & Irish case law. GhaLII holds the most complete free archive of Ghanaian judgments and legislation, in partnership with the Judicial Service of Ghana. Search goes straight to their own results; when available this site will present the text in-place using the site's proxy.
